No student should figure out their future alone

Preparing for CSEC and CAPE is hard enough. Knowing what to do after is harder. What should I study? Where can I apply? How do I pay for it? How do I even begin? Too many Guyanese students face those questions with very little guidance.

Next Step Guyana was started by Joshua Gulab and Aniyah Couchman, who remember that feeling well: the pressure, the uncertainty, and the quiet hope that someone would make things clearer.

Today we're building Guyana's first network of mentors who've walked the road before you: top performers, scholarship winners, university students and young professionals at home and abroad. Everything we offer is free, from Q&A events to honest advice and resources.
