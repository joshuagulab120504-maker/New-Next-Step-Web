Questions, answered

The things students and parents ask us most. Still unsure? Email us.

Getting help

Is Next Step Guyana really free?

Yes. Every session, conversation and resource is free. Our mentors volunteer because they want to give back to students in Guyana.

How do I get advice from a mentor?

Go to the Mentors page, filter by topic or search for a school or subject, and open a profile. Use the Contact button on their profile.

Some mentors share their contact details directly. For the rest, your message comes to our team and we introduce you.

What can mentors help with?

Choosing CSEC and CAPE subjects, study strategies, university options at UG, UWI and abroad, scholarship applications, essays, resumes, and figuring out a career path.

Who can use Next Step?

Any student in Guyana, or their parent, who is preparing for CSEC or CAPE, has finished them, or is making any decision about school, university or a career.

Q&A events

What happens at a Q&A event?

Each event focuses on one theme, like studying abroad, choosing CAPE subjects, or careers in law or medicine. A panel of mentors shares how they did it, then answers questions from everyone attending.

How do I join?

Upcoming events are listed on the Q&A Events page with the date, time and joining link. We also post every link on our WhatsApp channel and Instagram before the event starts.

I missed an event. Can I watch it later?

When an event is recorded, we post it on the Resources page under Videos.

About us

Is this a government programme?

No. Next Step Guyana is an independent initiative started by Guyanese students and run by volunteers. We're not tied to any government or private organisation.

Can my school or organisation partner with you?

Yes. If you're a school, youth group or education organisation, email us and we'll get back to you.
